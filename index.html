Questions about Sylheti translation

What is Sylheti?

Sylheti is the language of the Sylhet region in north-east Bangladesh. It is also the main home language of most British Bangladeshi families. It is close to Bengali but different enough that many Sylheti speakers need a Sylheti translator or interpreter, not a standard Bengali one.

Do you translate legal documents into Sylheti?

Yes. I have translated witness statements for the Court of Protection, the High Court and county courts, family court bundles, Cafcass Section 7 reports, care plans and parenting assessments from English into Sylheti.

Do you have a Sylheti voice-over sample?

Yes. You can play a 28-second sample of my Sylheti voice in the Services section of this page.

Can you transcribe Sylheti audio into English?

Yes. I have transcribed more than 8 hours of Sylheti and Bengali interviews, focus groups and recorded calls, delivered as timestamped transcripts with speakers labelled and an English translation under each line.

Do you also work in standard Bengali?

Yes. I am a native speaker of both Sylheti and Bengali, and I translate and interpret between English, Bengali and Urdu.
